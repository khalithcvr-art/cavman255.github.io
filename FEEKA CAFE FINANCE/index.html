🚀 Quick Start Guide

  1. Click "OPEN MAIN DASHBOARD" above
  2. Enter today's date (auto-filled)
  3. Add your expenses: Food, Dessert, Beverage, Other
  4. Add your sales: Food, Dessert, Beverage, Other
  5. Click "Add Daily Entry"
  6. View your profit instantly! 💰
