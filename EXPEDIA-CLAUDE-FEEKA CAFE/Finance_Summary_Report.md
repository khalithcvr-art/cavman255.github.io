# Feeka Cafe - Daily Finance System

## Overview
This comprehensive finance tracking system monitors daily expenses and sales for Feeka Cafe, including food, desserts, and beverages.

## Files Included

### 1. **Food_Menu.csv**
Complete menu listing with:
- Item names
- Categories (Coffee, Pastry, Dessert, Food, Beverage)
- Selling prices
- Cost prices
- **Total Items: 38**

#### Menu Breakdown:
- **Coffee Beverages:** 9 items ($2.50 - $4.00)
- **Pastries:** 4 items ($3.25 - $4.00)
- **Desserts:** 10 items ($2.50 - $5.50)
- **Food Items:** 10 items ($6.00 - $10.50)
- **Beverages:** 3 items ($1.50 - $3.50)

### 2. **Daily_Finance_Tracker.csv**
Daily tracking of:
- **Expenses by Category:**
  - Food Expenses
  - Dessert Expenses
  - Beverage Expenses
  - Total Daily Expenses

- **Revenue by Category:**
  - Food Sales
  - Dessert Sales
  - Beverage Sales
  - Total Daily Sales

- **Profitability:**
  - Daily Profit Calculation
  - Notes on special events/traffic

**Sample Period:** May 1-10, 2024
**Average Daily Sales:** $361.35
**Average Daily Expenses:** $101.32
**Average Daily Profit:** $260.03

## How to Use

### Daily Entry Process:
1. **Morning:** Record opening inventory status
2. **Throughout Day:** Log sales by category
3. **Evening:** 
   - Record all expenses incurred
   - Calculate profit margin
   - Note any special events or observations

### Weekly Review:
- Analyze which categories drive highest profit
- Identify slow-moving items
- Adjust pricing if needed
- Plan inventory for next week

### Monthly Summary:
- Calculate average daily sales
- Identify seasonal trends
- Analyze profit margins by category
- Plan marketing strategies

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Profit Margin by Category:
- **Coffee:** 50-65% profit margin
- **Pastries:** 35-45% profit margin
- **Desserts:** 40-55% profit margin
- **Food:** 30-45% profit margin
- **Beverages:** 50-75% profit margin

### Target Goals:
- **Daily Sales Target:** $350+
- **Overall Profit Margin:** 45-50%
- **Food Cost Ratio:** 25-30% of sales
- **Labor Cost Ratio:** 15-20% of sales
